02 / Journey

Nothing was
one straight line.

Some chapters ended. Some became foundations for the next.

01

Curiosity came first.

I grew up with a strong desire to learn and understand things, even when school did not always feel easy.

02

Fashion became an outlet.

In secondary school, fashion designing became something I genuinely wanted to explore. My parents supported the interest, giving me room to learn and create.

03

150 birds taught me business.

My brother and I had a mini poultry venture. I owned a smaller share but was closely involved in managing it. University eventually forced a pause, and we sold the birds.

04

LASU changed the direction.

I studied Human Resource Management at Lagos State University. Fashion and farming paused, but the experience added a professional foundation.

05

Work made the lessons practical.

My professional experience, including service at INEC, exposed me to documentation, records, administration, teamwork and real workplace responsibility.

06

Then I started building again.

Stillnotok became a creative and entrepreneurial expression. SNOKPay became a technology idea in development. Agriculture remained a future chapter.

07

The story is not finished.

I’m still learning, testing ideas, developing professionally and deciding what the next chapter should look like.
