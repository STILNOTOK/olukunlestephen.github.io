02 / Journey

The journey.

The experiences behind the person I am becoming — and the story that is still unfolding.

Perspective

Progress over perfection.

My journey has taught me that growth is rarely a straight line. There are seasons of learning, uncertainty, change and rebuilding.

Each experience has added another perspective: school taught me persistence, fashion taught me creativity, poultry taught me responsibility, university gave me professional direction, and entrepreneurship continues to teach me how to turn ideas into action.

Early years

A love for learning

Curiosity and the desire to understand how things work became part of who I am.

Before university

Discovering fashion and farming

I explored fashion designing and was also involved in managing a poultry venture with my brother, which grew to around 150 birds.

University

Human Resource Management

I studied Human Resource Management at Lagos State University and paused hands-on fashion and poultry work as my academic responsibilities increased.

Professional growth

Learning in the real world

My experience in HR and administration strengthened my organization, documentation, communication and workplace skills.

Entrepreneurship

Stillnotok

The fashion dream returned through Stillnotok — a brand built around authenticity, resilience, self-awareness and growth.

Today

Building beyond one industry

I am developing SNOKPay while keeping agriculture, technology, creativity, business and professional growth as important parts of my wider direction.

Long term

Returning to agriculture

I hope to build a palm-tree farm and livestock operation in the future, creating another practical venture from an interest that started early.
