The person

I’ve never had just one lane.

I am Olukunle Stephen Gbolahan, a Human Resource Management graduate from Lagos State University, entrepreneur and creative with interests across people, business, technology, fashion and agriculture.

My path has never been perfectly straight. I’ve learned through school, work, business, setbacks, experiments and the decision to keep going.

“You do not have to have everything figured out before you begin.”

Before the degree

There was fashion. And there was farming.

In secondary school, I developed a strong interest in fashion designing, with the support of my parents. Around the same period, my brother and I ran a small poultry venture with about 150 birds.

I had a smaller ownership share, but I was closely involved in managing the venture. University eventually made it difficult to remain available, so the birds were sold. The experience stayed with me.

Agriculture is still part of the picture. One day, I want to build serious palm-tree and livestock farming operations.

Today

Building across different worlds.

Human Resources gives me a professional foundation. Stillnotok gives me a creative voice. SNOKPay is a technology project in development. Agriculture remains a long-term ambition.

They may look different, but they share the same thing: I like turning ideas into something real.
