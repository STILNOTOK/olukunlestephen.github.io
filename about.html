Meet Olukunle Stephen.

A personal story shaped by curiosity, persistence, creativity and the desire to build.

The Person

Still becoming.

My name is Olukunle Stephen Gbolahan. I am a Human Resource Management graduate, entrepreneur and creative from Oyo, Nigeria, with my professional journey shaped in Lagos.

My story has never been a straight line. I have always been curious about learning, creating and finding ways to turn ideas into something real. Along the way, I have experienced uncertainty, academic struggles and moments when I questioned myself. Those experiences taught me persistence and the importance of continuing even when the path is not completely clear.

I believe you do not have to have everything figured out before you begin.

Where It Started

Fashion, farming and learning to build.

My interest in building things started early. While I was in secondary school, I discovered a passion for fashion designing. My parents supported that interest and gave me the opportunity to learn and explore the creative side of myself.

Agriculture has also been part of my journey. Before university, my brother and I had a poultry venture that grew to around 150 birds. Although I had a smaller ownership share, I was closely involved in managing the operation. When university made it difficult for me to remain available, we eventually had to sell the birds.

The poultry venture ended, but my interest in agriculture did not. I still hope to build agricultural ventures in the future, including a palm-tree farm and a livestock operation.

Education

Human Resource Management.

I studied Human Resource Management at Lagos State University (LASU). University gave me more than an academic qualification; it strengthened my understanding of people, organizations, responsibility and professional life.

My education also became part of a wider journey. While fashion and agriculture remained interests close to me, I began developing a professional direction in Human Resources and administration.
